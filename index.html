Our Story

Hi, I'm Swati and this is my kitchen.

The Homestyle Bakery began in Swati's home kitchen in Colaba, with the kind of food she was already making for family, friends and the people who kept saying, "You should really sell this."

It started with small batches, familiar flavours and a simple belief: food tastes better when it is made with care. The first orders were never about building a big bakery. They were about sending out something fresh, comforting and honest from one home to another.

One of the first bakes people fell in love with was the Vada Pav Bun: soft, nostalgic and filled with the flavours of Mumbai in a way that felt both new and instantly familiar.

Today, The Homestyle Bakery has grown into a menu of fresh sourdough, stuffed buns, chikkis, snacks, pasta and sauces, but the heart of it is still the same. Small batches. Good ingredients. Food that feels like it came from someone's kitchen, because it did.

Good to know

Questions we get asked

Where do you deliver?

Across South Mumbai: Colaba, Cuffe Parade, Churchgate, Fort, Marine Lines, Worli and Bandra. If you're somewhere else, message us and we'll let you know if we can reach you.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your order on this page and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order opens in WhatsApp, ready to send. You can also message us directly on +91 96191 22581. We confirm the total, delivery slot and payment in the chat.

Is there a minimum order or delivery charge?

The minimum order is ₹500. Delivery is ₹50, and free on orders of ₹1,500 and above.

Do your prices include GST?

Menu prices exclude GST. We'll share your final total on WhatsApp before you pay.

Is everything vegetarian? Do you make Jain food?

Yes, ours is a 100% vegetarian kitchen. Jain versions of many items are available on request. Mention it in your order notes and we'll confirm which ones.

What about allergies?

Our kitchen works with wheat (gluten), dairy, nuts, seeds and sesame. If you have an allergy, please ask us about specific items before you order.

Can you cater parties, office orders or festive gifting?

Yes. Message us with the date, the occasion and roughly how many people, and we'll suggest a spread. For bigger orders, reach out a few days ahead so we can plan the bake.
